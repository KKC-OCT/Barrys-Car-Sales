ENQUIRY.HTML AFTER FIX

Keyboard and form

Every field now has a proper label linked to it: Vehicle, Full name, Email, Telephone, Message, the checkbox, and the Email and Phone radios. Clicking the text now selects the checkbox and radios. WAVE shows 8 form labels and no missing label errors.
“Preferred contact method” is now grouped in a <fieldset> with a <legend>.
Email is now type="email" and Telephone is type="tel".
Required fields are marked with a red * and a “* Required fields” note, and they have the required attribute. The marker is a symbol plus text, so it no longer relies on colour alone.
Leaving a required field empty now gives an error (“Please fill out this field”), and a bad email gets “Please include an ‘@’...”. These are the browser’s built-in messages.
Submitting a correct form now shows “Thank you! Your enquiry has been submitted successfully.” and clears the form. Before, Enter just wiped everything with no feedback.
The confirmation is announced to screen readers (role="status", aria-live="polite"), and WAVE shows it as 1 ARIA live region.
The message box is no longer pre-filled. The hint is now placeholder text, so nothing needs deleting.
The button now says “Submit Enquiry” instead of “SUBMIT”.
The focused field shows a dark outline, as in the Full name screenshot.
Arriving from a car link on the home page now pre-selects that vehicle in the dropdown.

Layout

The form is now in a box with full-width fields and consistent spacing. The checkbox and radios sit next to their labels, and the form lines up.

Navigation

The nav is Home, About, Enquire, matching the other pages. “Enquiry” is now “Enquire”.
“Enquire” is bold with a line under it, and aria-current="page" is set, so it’s clear which page I’m on.

Extras

Added a privacy note with a link to the Privacy Notice, under the form.
